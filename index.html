01 / ABSTRACT

From actions to coherent trajectories.

We study controllable video generation where character and camera actions must occur at precise moments.

Our approach encodes an action schedule as language instructions aligned with individual video latents. Directed attention binds each instruction to its corresponding future state, enabling an action-controllable model without a separate action-specific interface.

KEY IDEATemporal language conditions provide a lightweight interface for frame-aligned world control.
